Revenue Management Simulator

Optimize airline revenue by managing seat prices and availability across different booking classes.

Key Concepts:

  • Booking Classes: Different fare categories (e.g., Economy, Business) with varying prices.
  • Capacity Control: Manage how many seats are allocated to each booking class.
  • Overbooking: Selling more seats than available to compensate for no-shows.
  • Revenue: Total income generated from ticket sales.

How to Use:

  • Explore: Rotate, pan, and zoom the aircraft model.
  • Simulation: Run booking scenarios to observe outcomes.
  • Strategies: Fine-tune prices and capacities to maximize revenue.
